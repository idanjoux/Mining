Process

Four steps from
interest to closing.

We've built a structured acquisition process that protects buyers, ensures transparency, and moves efficiently from first contact to final close.

01

Execute NDA

Sign our standard Non-Disclosure & Non-Circumvention Agreement via DocuSign. The agreement protects both parties and is governed under Ontario law. It takes under five minutes to complete. Once executed, you're cleared to receive full property documentation.

02

Receive the full package

Access the complete information memorandum for any property of interest — including geology reports, independent lab assay certificates, concession title documentation, financial modeling, infrastructure summary, and the full asking price and deal structure. Nothing is withheld at this stage.

03

Site visit & due diligence

We facilitate a supervised visit to the physical property and arrange a direct introduction to the mine owner's technical team. Buyers may bring their own geologist or third-party verifier. All additional testing results are added to the data room in real time.

04

Offer & close

Submit a binding offer through our standard Letter of Intent template. We manage all negotiations between buyer and seller, coordinate legal review, and work with both parties' counsel to reach final closing. Our 10% broker fee is earned at closing and applies to the final agreed transaction price.

FAQ

Common questions

Do I need to be a Canadian citizen or company to purchase?
No. We regularly work with international buyers from the US, Europe, and Asia-Pacific. The properties are in Mexico and acquisition is governed by Mexican concession law. We work alongside local Mexican legal counsel for all transactions.
What does the NDA restrict me from doing?
The NDA prevents circumvention — you cannot approach the mine owner directly, share confidential data with third parties outside your acquisition team, or use the data for purposes other than evaluating the acquisition. Standard terms for this type of transaction.
How long does the process typically take from NDA to close?
Most transactions complete within 60–120 days. The NDA and information package stages move quickly. Site visits typically add 2–4 weeks depending on travel logistics. Negotiation and legal close vary by complexity.
Are the assay grades verified by independent labs?
Yes. Every mineral grade published in our portfolio is backed by a certificate from an independent, accredited laboratory. We do not publish seller-provided grades without independent confirmation. Lab certificates are included in the full information package.
What is the broker fee structure?
Mineora charges a 10% broker fee on all transactions, payable at closing. There are no upfront fees for reviewing the portfolio or executing the NDA. The fee is payable by the buyer on the final agreed transaction price.
Can I view a property before signing an NDA?
We can share the publicly visible listing data — property name, location, mineral type, and headline metrics. Full documentation, exact location coordinates, lab certificates, and pricing are disclosed only post-NDA execution.
