The structure

Loana Vanessa Danjoux

Founder & Principal Broker

Mineora was founded on a straightforward premise: the mining brokerage market in Mexico was full of opportunity but structurally broken. Properties with real value sat undiscovered because sellers lacked access to serious international capital, and buyers had no trusted intermediary to verify claims and govern transactions.

Loana Vanessa Danjoux built Mineora to close that gap — bringing Ontario legal infrastructure, independent lab verification, and direct owner relationships together into a single brokerage model built for qualified buyers.

Operating through 1000175534 Ontario Ltd., Mineora holds exclusive and semi-exclusive broker agreements across a portfolio of ten active properties in Mexico's primary mining corridors — Chihuahua, Sinaloa, Durango, Sonora, and Michoacán.

Every property in the portfolio was sourced through direct owner relationships, not public listings. Every grade was verified by an independent, accredited laboratory before the property was added. Every transaction is governed under Ontario law with full legal documentation in place before any confidential data changes hands.

Mineora is not a marketplace. It is a structured brokerage with a finite, curated portfolio — built for buyers who understand that premium access requires premium process.
