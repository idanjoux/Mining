Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Company

This Privacy Policy applies to 1000175534 Ontario Ltd., operating as Mineora ("we," "us," or "our"), a company incorporated in the Province of Ontario, Canada.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including name, email address, company name, and investor type when you complete our contact form or execute our NDA. We may also collect standard web analytics data including pages visited and approximate geographic region.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information solely to respond to your inquiry, send you the portfolio documentation you requested, facilitate the NDA execution process, and communicate relevant updates about our active properties. We do not use your information for marketing to third parties.

4. NDA Data

Information shared in connection with an executed NDA is handled in accordance with the confidentiality terms of that agreement. Both parties' obligations under the NDA govern the treatment of confidential information exchanged during the acquisition process.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties except as necessary to facilitate a transaction you have initiated (e.g., providing your information to a mine owner as part of an introduction process, with your consent), or as required by applicable law.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by applicable law. Contact us to request deletion of your information.

7. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of the Province of Ontario and the federal laws of Canada applicable therein.

8. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, please contact us through the form at mineora.ca/contact.
