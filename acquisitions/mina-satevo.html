Overview

Mina Satevó is a lithium pegmatite exploration property located in the Satevó municipality of Chihuahua state, one of Mexico's premier mining jurisdictions. The property covers 1,040 hectares of titled concession area and hosts a confirmed lithium-cesium-rubidium (LCT) pegmatite system.

Independent assay results confirm lithium grades of 2,452 parts per million across sampled zones — placing the property firmly within economic interest thresholds for strategic battery-metal acquisition.

Geology & Mineralogy

The property hosts a classic LCT (lithium-cesium-tantalum) pegmatite system with confirmed spodumene and lepidolite mineralisation. Surface sampling across 12 zones has returned consistent lithium grades above 2,000 ppm, with cesium and rubidium credits providing additional economic value.

  • Spodumene and lepidolite confirmed in surface outcrop
  • 12 sampling zones — 10 above 2,000 ppm Li
  • Cesium grades up to 340 ppm Cs confirmed
  • Rubidium mineralisation present throughout pegmatite body
  • Depth extent not yet determined — drilling ongoing
Infrastructure & Access

The property is accessible via paved highway to within 4 km, with existing dirt road access to the main zone. Water access confirmed on-site. Grid power available within 8 km. The Satevó municipality has an established mining services ecosystem.

Deal Structure

The asking price of USD $24,000,000 represents a direct acquisition of the concession title and all associated exploration data, sampling results, and lab certificates. A 10% broker fee applies at closing. Full financial model and concession documentation available post-NDA.
