Overview

Proyecto Baluarte is a gold-silver exploration property in the Concordia municipality, located at the Sinaloa–Durango border — a zone that captures the mineralised corridor connecting two of Mexico's premier gold-producing states. The property carries an inferred resource of 5,959 ounces of gold, confirmed by independent assay.

Geology & Resource

The Baluarte project is situated along a structural corridor that intersects known gold-silver vein systems in the Sierra Madre Occidental. The 5,959 oz Au inferred figure is based on surface sampling and shallow trenching, with the resource open at depth and along strike — suggesting meaningful upside for a systematic exploration program.

  • 5,959 oz Au inferred — independent assay confirmed
  • Open at depth — no deep drilling completed
  • Multiple vein targets identified — resource covers primary zone only
  • Silver credits present across sampled zones
  • Accessible terrain — road access to main zone
Strategic Value

For a buyer with exploration appetite, Proyecto Baluarte offers a meaningful resource base at exploration-stage pricing, with the upside of a district-scale exploration corridor that has not been fully tested. The Concordia area has produced significant gold historically, and this property sits within a geologically favorable structural position.
