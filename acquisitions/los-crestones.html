Overview

Los Crestones is a gold-silver exploration property in the Badiraguato municipality of Sinaloa, backed by a full NI 43-101 technical report — the Canadian standard for mineral property disclosure, widely regarded as the most rigorous in the global mining industry.

NI 43-101 certification distinguishes this property in the Mexican exploration landscape, providing buyers with a level of technical rigor and independent verification typically associated with Canadian-listed mining companies.

NI 43-101 Certification

The National Instrument 43-101 is a Canadian securities regulation governing the disclosure of scientific and technical information about mineral properties. A compliant technical report requires a Qualified Person (QP) — a certified geoscientist — to independently verify and sign off on all resource estimates and technical claims.

  • Full NI 43-101 technical report complete
  • Qualified Person (QP) certification — independent geoscientist
  • Mineral resource estimate included in report
  • All grades independently verified per NI 43-101 requirements
  • Full report provided post-NDA
Location

Badiraguato municipality in Sinaloa is located in the Sierra Madre Occidental mountain range — a prolific gold-silver mining belt extending through western Mexico. The region has a long history of productive mining and an established understanding of local mining law and concession management.
