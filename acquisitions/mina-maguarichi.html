Overview

Mina Maguarichi is an active gold-silver producing property in the Maguarichi municipality of Chihuahua, currently in advanced LOI (Letter of Intent) stage. This property is structured as a royalty acquisition — a revenue-based interest in the producing mine rather than a full concession purchase.

This is a rare opportunity to acquire a royalty position on an active producing asset in one of Mexico's established gold corridors. Full structure details, production metrics, and pricing are disclosed post-NDA.

Deal Structure — Royalty

Unlike a standard concession acquisition, Mina Maguarichi is structured as a royalty transaction. The buyer acquires a defined percentage royalty on production revenue from the active mine — providing ongoing cash flow from day one without the operational requirements of direct mine ownership.

  • Revenue royalty on active gold-silver production
  • Mine currently in production — no ramp-up required
  • LOI stage — advanced negotiation with mine owner
  • Royalty percentage and term disclosed post-NDA
  • Independent production audit available to qualified buyers
Why Maguarichi

The Maguarichi district of Chihuahua has a long mining history with established artisanal and semi-industrial operations. The municipality is accessible, with established relationships between local communities and mining operators — reducing social licence risk.
