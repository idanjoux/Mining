Overview

Zahav Tov is a development-stage gold-silver property located in the Mulatos district of Sonora — one of Mexico's premier gold mining jurisdictions, home to several producing mines operated by major international companies. The property carries an inferred resource of 82,453 ounces of gold, confirmed by independent technical reporting.

At an asking price of USD $33,000,000, the acquisition represents approximately $400/oz in-situ gold on the confirmed resource — with upside from untested extensions.

Resource & Geology

The 150-hectare concession hosts a confirmed gold mineralised system with 82,453 ounces of inferred gold resource. The mineralisation model is consistent with the Mulatos district's characteristic low-sulfidation epithermal gold systems.

  • 82,453 oz Au inferred resource — independent technical report
  • Low-sulfidation epithermal system — established metallurgical pathway
  • Silver credits across resource area
  • Drill program data complete — full logs available post-NDA
  • Development-stage — permitting options assessed
District Context

The Mulatos district in Sonora is an established gold mining corridor. Infrastructure in the region is well-developed, with experienced local workforce, regional processing history, and established relationships with Mexican federal mining authorities.
