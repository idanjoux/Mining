01

Opportunity Overview

Project Zahav is a gold and silver exploration asset located east of Hermosillo in Sonora State — Mexico's most prolific gold-producing state, home to assets operated by Fresnillo, Agnico Eagle, First Majestic, and dozens of active junior explorers. The property is held by Minecrons SA de CV, whose principals have engaged SeisGlobe Geoservices to conduct a combined high-resolution geophysical program using IP, magnetic, and well-integration methods to precisely characterize the gold-silver reserve structure and define priority drill targets.

The property has existing borehole (well) data that will be integrated with the geophysical surveys to build a subsurface model of the deposit. The technical program — proposed by SeisGlobe (Reference LGG-0052-24, November 2024) — employs multi-method geophysical characterization including induced polarization (IP), magnetic surveys, and 3D geological modelling to identify drill-ready targets before the first hole is spudded.

The asset represents an investment entry at a geophysics-program stage — between initial geological identification and first drilling — which historically offers the highest risk-adjusted return per exploration dollar for informed investors with technical capacity to evaluate the results.

Sonora is the most actively explored state in Mexico for gold and silver. With gold at $4,784/oz (May 2026) and Sonora's proven district-scale endowment, Project Zahav is entering the market at an ideal time for exploration capital deployment.

02

Key Competitive Advantages

Sonora — Premier Jurisdiction
Sonora is Mexico's most active gold exploration state, with a long history of major discoveries and active operations by Fresnillo, Agnico Eagle, First Majestic, and numerous juniors. The regulatory environment is experienced, the geological framework is well-understood, and infrastructure from previous exploration cycles is widely available.
Geophysics-Guided Targeting
Rather than exploratory drilling, Project Zahav is employing SeisGlobe's combined IP, magnetic, and well-integration geophysical workflow to define high-confidence drill targets. This approach substantially improves hit probability and reduces the cost per discovery interval compared to blind drill programs.
Existing Well Data Integration
Pre-existing borehole data from the property will be integrated into the SeisGlobe geophysical model, providing a calibration point for subsurface interpretation that most early-stage exploration projects do not have. This reduces interpretation uncertainty and accelerates the path from geophysics to drill-ready targets.
Proximity to Hermosillo
The asset is located east of Hermosillo, Sonora's capital and industrial hub, providing straightforward access to logistics infrastructure, technical services, and labour markets. Proximity to a major city significantly reduces exploration operating costs versus remote Sonora properties.
Early Entry Valuation
Geophysics-stage assets offer the most leverage per exploration dollar — between geological identification and first drilling, the market has not yet assigned discovery value to the property. An incoming partner or acquirer gains the maximum proportion of any future resource value for the minimum initial capital commitment.
Experienced Operator
Minecrons SA de CV brings operational experience in the Sonora mining sector and has structured the exploration program with SeisGlobe — an established Mexican geophysical consultancy — to ensure the technical program meets international exploration standards for subsequent capital raising or asset monetization.
