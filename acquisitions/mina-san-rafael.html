Overview

Mina San Rafael is a silver-zinc-lead polymetallic exploration property in the Urique municipality of Chihuahua — a deep canyon district known historically for high-grade silver production. The property covers 620 hectares of titled concession and has returned silver grades of 18.4 oz/t Ag from independent assay.

Geology & Grade

The property hosts a classic polymetallic vein system with high-grade silver as the primary value driver, complemented by zinc and lead credits. The Urique corridor of Chihuahua is geologically analogous to Mexico's historic silver belt systems, with vein structures extending to significant depth.

  • 18.4 oz/t Ag peak grade — independent assay certified
  • Zinc and lead credits across mineralised zones
  • 620 hectares — substantial exploration footprint
  • Vein system confirmed — multiple structures mapped
  • Urique district — established silver mining heritage
Urique, Chihuahua

The Urique municipality sits within the Copper Canyon region of Chihuahua — a dramatic and minerally rich landscape that has yielded silver, gold, and base metals for centuries. The area has established mining infrastructure and an experienced local workforce familiar with vein-hosted polymetallic systems.
