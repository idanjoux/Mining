Overview

La Sabila is the flagship property in the Mineora portfolio — an advanced-stage gold-silver-copper deposit in Caracuaro, Michoacán, carrying a confirmed resource of 954,000 ounces of gold. At 174.5 hectares of titled concession area, this is a near-development asset priced at USD $60,000,000.

With nearly one million ounces of confirmed gold resource, La Sabila represents a rare large-scale acquisition opportunity in Mexico at a price that implies approximately $63/oz on the confirmed resource base.

Resource & Scale

The 954,000 oz Au resource places La Sabila in the category of meaningful mid-tier gold deposits — sufficient scale to support a standalone mining operation or serve as a strategic addition to an existing producer's portfolio.

  • 954,000 oz Au confirmed resource — independent assay
  • Silver and copper credits present throughout resource area
  • 174.5 hectares — full titled concession
  • Advanced stage — extensive exploration data package complete
  • Multiple zones — primary resource and satellite targets
  • Michoacán jurisdiction — established mining framework
Advanced Stage — What's Included

The advanced stage designation reflects the volume and quality of technical work completed. The information package includes full drill logs, assay certificates from multiple campaigns, geological models, metallurgical test results, infrastructure assessment, and a preliminary economic analysis.

Deal Structure

USD $60,000,000 for direct acquisition of the concession title and all associated technical data, resource models, drill logs, and certificates. At this price level, buyer and seller negotiate final structure directly with Mineora facilitating. Broker fee of 10% applies at closing. Full technical data room opens post-NDA.
