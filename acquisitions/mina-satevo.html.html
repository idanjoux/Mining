— 02 / Investment thesis

A market where adding one credible alternative supplier is itself a re-rating event.

The structural setup

Cesium has only two active producing operations globally — Tanco in Manitoba and Bikita in Zimbabwe. Both are operated by China's Sinomine Resource Group, which controls effectively the entire cesium formate market.

Rubidium is produced almost exclusively as a by-product of Chinese lepidolite mines. Non-Chinese supply is, in practical terms, zero.

When Japan's historical cesium supply from Bikita was interrupted following the 2022 Sinomine acquisition, the country was unable to substitute. There is no spot market.

The opportunity

Cesium and rubidium are now on the critical minerals lists of the United States, Canada, the European Union, and Japan. Defense, GPS timing, deep-well drilling, atomic clocks, and 5G synchronization all depend on uninterrupted Cs and Rb supply.

Western governments are actively funding non-Chinese alternatives through grants, offtakes, and friend-shoring programs. A Chihuahua project — clay/zeolite host rock, Mexican concession in good standing, US-aligned trade framework — is geographically and politically aligned.

Lithium is intentionally not the lead. Mexican lithium is reserved to the State and will require a partnership structure when its time comes. For now, Cs and Rb are the cleanly transactable story.

— 04 / The asset

Project Elisa, in brief.

What sits on the ground

A concession in Chihuahua state with 350 permitted hectares for metals exploration and an additional 3,000 hectares of contiguous land identified for further exploration, hosting alkali-bearing clay and zeolite assemblages with confirmed Cs, Rb, and Li mineralization.

Two ALS Chemex certified analyses (April 2020 and February 2021), conducted by the standard ME-MS61 ICP-MS method, document peak grades of 443 ppm Cs, 280 ppm Rb, and 3,540 ppm Li across multiple samples.

The concession is held by Zeolita de Chihuahua S.A. de C.V. and is in good standing, with certified titles available to qualified buyers under NDA.
