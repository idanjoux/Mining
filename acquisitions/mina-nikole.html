Overview

Mina Nikole is a high-grade polymetallic gold-silver-copper property straddling the Durango–Sinaloa border in the Tamazula district — one of Mexico's most historically productive gold corridors. The property spans 2,186 hectares of titled concession and has returned a peak gold grade of 42.25 g/t Au, confirmed by independent laboratory assay.

This is one of the highest-grade gold properties currently available for acquisition in Mexico. Asking price and full deal structure are disclosed post-NDA execution.

Geology & Grade

The property hosts high-grade gold mineralisation within a mesothermal vein system typical of the Sierra Madre Occidental gold belt. Multiple vein structures have been identified, with the primary zone returning the headline 42.25 g/t Au figure from independent assay.

  • Peak gold grade: 42.25 g/t Au — independent lab certification
  • Silver credits confirmed across all assayed zones
  • Copper mineralisation present in depth extensions
  • Multiple vein structures mapped — primary and secondary systems
  • Phase 1 exploration data package complete
Location & Access

Situated in the Tamazula district at the Durango–Sinaloa border — a region with deep mining heritage and established infrastructure. Road access to property. Proximity to regional processing facilities adds optionality to the development scenario.
