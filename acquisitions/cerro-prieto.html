Overview

Cerro Prieto is a gold-copper porphyry exploration property in the Álamos municipality of Sonora — a historic silver and gold mining town at the edge of the Sierra Madre Occidental. The property covers 2,400 hectares of titled concession and hosts a confirmed copper-gold porphyry system, the style of deposit responsible for the world's largest copper and gold mines.

Porphyry System

Copper-gold porphyry deposits are among the most economically significant in the mining world — large, bulk-tonnage systems that form the backbone of global copper supply and major gold producers. Cerro Prieto hosts confirmed porphyry-style mineralisation across the 2,400-hectare concession, with systematic exploration upside.

  • Confirmed Cu-Au porphyry system — geological mapping complete
  • 2,400 hectares — district-scale land position
  • Gold and copper grades confirmed by independent assay
  • Porphyry systems are bulk-tonnage — scale potential significant
  • Álamos district — established gold-silver mining heritage
  • Exploration upside — system extent not fully tested
Álamos, Sonora

Álamos is a colonial mining town in southern Sonora with centuries of precious metals production history. The surrounding district hosts a variety of deposit styles, with the local geology favorable for porphyry copper-gold systems at depth beneath the Sierra Madre range.

Pricing

Given the porphyry nature of the system and associated scale potential, pricing is disclosed exclusively post-NDA. Full geological mapping, assay data, and technical assessment included in the information package.
